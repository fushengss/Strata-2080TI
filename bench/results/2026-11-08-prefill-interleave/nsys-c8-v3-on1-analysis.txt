Material limits
Node-level profiling is intrusive here: explicit PROFILER_OVERHEAD intervals union602.082ms, and graph API timing includes unseparated driver/CUPTI costs. The helper cohort ran under instrumentation; no causal speedup, normal-service utilization percentage, or future optimization gain follows from these timings. Use the unprofiled A/B and incremental workload for deployment decisions.
Nsight reports6incomplete CUPTI events dropped when GPU timestamps were not retrieved by profiling stop, plus incomplete-CUDA/OSRT warnings. The exact locations of those6events are unknown. Observed busy union is therefore a lower bound if any missing work intersects the window; an observed gap is not absolute proof of hardware idle. Counts from our extraction match all exported tables, so our transport/parser did not discard recorded events.
CPU sampling and context switches were intentionally disabled. CUDA-event trace is off. No CPU scheduling attribution, complete cross-stream dependency graph, or absolute GPU utilization claim is supported. This short C8 decode workload also does not demonstrate the long-prefill overlap path.
Use event timestamps normalized by the verified export option and helper SSE wall time. ANALYSIS_DETAILS header start/stop differs from GPU event epochs in this export, so it was not used to align or choose the measurement window.
