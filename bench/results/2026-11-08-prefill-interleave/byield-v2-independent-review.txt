Two concrete false-fail findings were sent to the author/root for a separate v3 package before BYIELD GPU qualification:

1. With a nonzero reader begin.from caused by a turn/checkpoint boundary, a correct yield would be rejected. Validate0<stopped_at<tokens, derive yield_at=from+stopped_at, and match park/resume at yield_at. Preserve chunk and original segment endpoint checks. Add nonzero-from positive and wrong-absolute-position negative cases.

2. Final cache-zero requirement is wrong for the yielded long request. byield_smoke.py:107 calls admission.normal_gates(cold=True) for every request. Successful partial park/resume can report positive final cached_tokens/cache_n. Engine DONE reports reused=resume and fresh=n-resume (generate.cpp:19754–19778). server.py:2488–2489 continues a yielded admission without collecting a first-leg reused0; request_timings:6847–6851 derives final cache from resume/read. reused0 is for solo/slot handback, not this BYIELD branch. Preserve cold gates for independent non-yielding peers/short request; permit and report the long request's final resume-cache metadata, while retaining its new independent fixture and native initial-read/actual park/resume evidence. Keep all logical prompt/completion/total counts, complete output hashes, finish and timing-accounting gates. Existing records_equal compares logical counts rather than requiring identical cache metadata, which is appropriate.
