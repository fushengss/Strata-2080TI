3. C4 does not supply eight independent target windows.

  /Users/fu/project/HAI/Strata-decode-head/src/program/generate.cpp:14840
    GS=batch/groups=8/8=1. The workload has four active resident slots/groups.
  /Users/fu/project/HAI/Strata-decode-head/src/program/generate.cpp:15232
    A group cannot start a second target window while its first is in flight.
  /Users/fu/project/HAI/Strata-decode-head/src/program/generate.cpp:15233
    Stage 0 also waits for that group's MTP proposal chain to become ready.
  /Users/fu/project/HAI/Strata-decode-head/src/program/generate.cpp:15164
    A group advances through stages only after the prior stage completes;
    terminal verdict and commit precede its next speculative step.

Ignoring offloaded head/draft activity, four active groups can occupy at most four
target stages at once. Additional empty groups do not create independent work.
This is not a 50% bound on nvidia-smi utilization: draft/head kernels, prefill,
copies, unequal stage times and waiting kernels contribute differently.

Prefill also has finite fill/drain cost. An illustrative model with eight equal
chunks and eight equal stages has average prefill stage occupancy
8/(8+8-1)=53.3% over the whole prompt. Here the last chunk is shorter, stages have
unequal work, and decode overlaps, so this formula is neither a prediction of
the observed ~53.6% activity nor a bound on combined utilization. It explains
why a finite request cannot spend its entire lifetime at a full pipeline.

The GPU CSV samples every ~0.5 s and nvidia-smi activity itself uses rolling
windows. Around the 1.8 s client gap several cards fall to 0-2% sampled activity
and power is mostly ~75-98 W, consistent with broad idle time. These samples
cannot resolve the exact park/drain/copy subphases or achieved FLOP/bandwidth use.
