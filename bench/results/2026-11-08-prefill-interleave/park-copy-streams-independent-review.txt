Park copy streams and shared snapshot helpers — independent feasibility review
Source bf5a8e5b24b1973efbae747a109f5a970b25e09d, local read-only review. No implementation, remote action or GPU test.

Copy streams are thread-local, not a shared per-device singleton
src/core/conversation_copy.hpp:71–73 owns thread_local ThreadCopyHandles; each thread has its own per-device stream/event vectors. private_stream83–103 checks out a handle under the process pool mutex or creates a nonblocking stream, then the active thread exclusively owns that handle. The process-wide CopyHandlePool32–40 only retains free handles; thread destruction60–68 returns them. Thus different workers can have different streams on the same GPU. copy_nonblocking140–151 synchronizes this thread's stream unless queued=true; order_after_queued orders this thread's stream. A main-thread synchronization of its own TLS stream does not drain another worker's stream. A main-thread copy_stream_ready check108+ does not establish worker readiness when main still owns the handle. Prewarm/check on the actual worker or explicitly transfer owned handles, and drain any queued copies before thread lifetime ends.

This does not make whole snapshot_save safe for eight concurrent stage workers
- conversation_snapshot.cpp:215–229 ParkAsync is global. conversation_park_async_begin596–604 joins pending under a local mutex, then mutates active/items/used/slot without a transaction-wide lock. Those resets occur even when PARK_ASYNC is disabled. conversation_kv_save314 and325–370 reads or mutates that global state. Two snapshot transactions can race despite private copy streams.
- conversation_snapshot.cpp:124–136 VramKv is global and explicitly documented host-thread-only; slot lookup/take/release mutate its vectors/tags without a global operation lock. KvSaveTrace251–252 is global and concurrently reset/updated when tracing is enabled.
- conversation_state.cpp:172–187 CkptAsync and VramSlots are global. checkpoint_save306+ can reallocate stage/host/stream and update device/capacity outside the mutex; the mutex only covers pending future publication/join, not the whole save.
- CkptGather222–239 is a global scratch allocation with an explicit mutex around gathering. This part serializes correctly, but different devices can repeatedly replace its buffers, and it does not protect the other helpers.
- snapshot_save528–531 joins previous global checkpoint/park work and calls sync(error), whose implementation at32–33 is cudaDeviceSynchronize. Current generate.cpp12335–12350 captures the main state then later stages serially, with another explicit device synchronization per later stage. These are potential wait/driver-lock points; a private stream alone does not remove them.

Feasible next scope to audit
One background snapshot worker that captures stages sequentially may overlap the host's strictly resident decode pump, provided the pump cannot invoke checkpoint/park/restore/cache-eviction or other shared-helper consumers concurrently, has no aliases to the snapshot source, and owns separate error/results state. Preserve joins and image/lifetime publication; allocations and device synchronization still require a mapped-doorbell/PLE dependency audit. This is a candidate design scope, not a proof of safety or performance. Eight concurrent full snapshot_save calls require isolated operation contexts or a complete serialization contract, and are not justified by TLS copy streams.
