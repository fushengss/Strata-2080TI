Why current events are insufficient:

It is a particular commit's completion, not a fence for Prefill streams or MTP. Other commit paths exist (:3895, commit_slots:4849); a last verifier output event is not a universal final state event.

KvDrain(:3284) protects its own KV-copy stream only.
- conversation_copy.hpp:248-273 order_after_queued records a private COPY completion event and makes the legacy stream wait. Its direction is copy->legacy, the reverse of producer->snapshot; copy_stream_ready:108 only proves handle availability, not data readiness.
